⚽ SQUADFORGE — RULES (v0.4)
Private Fantasy Premier League for our group

Copy/paste this note for WhatsApp.
(We can edit and re-send anytime — changes apply from an agreed gameweek.)

————————————
SQUAD & TRANSFERS
————————————
• Squad of 15: 2 GK · 5 DEF · 5 MID · 3 ATT
• Budget £100m · max 3 players per Premier League club
• GW1: unlimited free transfers
• After GW1: +1 free transfer each GW (banked, max 5)
• Extra transfers beyond FTs: −4 hit each
• Deadline locks transfers, lineup, and chips for that GW

————————————
LINEUP
————————————
• Each GW set your Starting XI, bench order, Captain (C) and Vice (V)
• Captain scores ×2
• If captain plays 0 minutes → vice gets the armband
• If someone in your XI doesn't play, autosubs can bring a bench player in (like FPL)

————————————
APPEARANCE (all positions)
————————————
• 2 pts if they play 60'+ minutes
• 2 pts if they play ANY minutes AND get a goal or assist
• 1 pt if they played but none of the above
• 0 if they didn’t play

(Goal/assist points are EXTRA on top of appearance.)

————————————
EVERYONE — CARDS / MISTAKES
————————————
• Yellow card: −1
• Red card: −3
• Own goal: −2

————————————
GOALKEEPER
————————————
• Appearance: as above
• Clean sheet: +4
• Goals conceded: −1 for every 2 goals conceded
• Saves: +1 for every 3 saves
• Goal: +6 · Assist: +3
• Penalty saved: +5 · Penalty missed: −2

————————————
DEFENDER
————————————
Base
• Appearance · Goal +6 · Assist +3 · Clean sheet +4
• Goals conceded: −1 for every 2

Defensive extras (thresholds)
• 5+ tackles → +2
• 4+ interceptions → +1
• 3+ blocks → +1
• 10+ clearances → +1
• Goal-line clearance / save on the line → +1

⚠️ Max +4 from defensive extras per GW.
Priority if over cap: goal-line → tackles → interceptions → blocks → clearances

————————————
MIDFIELDER
————————————
Base
• Appearance · Goal +5 · Assist +3 · Clean sheet +1

Extras
• 4+ key passes → +2
• 5+ tackles → +1
• 4+ interceptions → +1

⚠️ Max +3 from MID extras per GW.
Priority: key passes → tackles → interceptions

————————————
ATTACKER
————————————
Base
• Appearance · Goal +4 · Assist +3

Extras
• 3+ shots on target → +2
• 6+ shots → +1

⚠️ Max +3 from ATT extras per GW.
Priority: shots on target → shots

————————————
SCOUTING BONUS (+2)
————————————
If ONLY YOU own a player in our league, and that player:
• scores, OR gets an assist, OR
• keeps a clean sheet as GK/DEF
→ you get +2 scouting for that player that GW.

————————————
TECHNICAL DIRECTOR (not a chip)
————————————
• Shown on Squad & XI pitch · bottom-right corner
• Pick a real PL club ANYTIME you don’t already have an active TD window
• That club then scores for exactly 3 gameweeks (then pick again)
• Win +3 · Draw +1 · Loss −1
• Locked during those 3 GWs (no mid-window swap)
• Cannot pick the same club as your previous TD window
• Wildcard is separate

————————————
CHIPS (1 active chip per GW)
————————————
You get these once per season (Wildcard ×2):

1) WILDCARD (×2 per season)
   Rebuild with unlimited free transfers until the next deadline. Squad stays.
   • 1st Wildcard: available from the start
   • 2nd Wildcard: unlocks from GW20

2) FREE HIT (×1)
   Change your whole team for ONE gameweek only. After that GW, your old team returns.

3) BENCH BOOST (×1)
   Bench players’ points ALSO count that GW.

4) TRIPLE CAPTAIN (×1)
   Captain scores ×3 that GW (instead of ×2).

5) SUPER SUB (×1) — SquadForge special
   Before deadline, pick ONE bench player.
   If they play (or autosub in) that GW, their points count at ×2.
   Chip is used up even if they don’t play.
   Does not stack with Triple Captain. Can’t combine with Bench Boost same GW.

————————————
IN THE APP
————————————
• Squad = ownership + transfers + season stats + next fixtures
• Lineup = XI / bench / C / V · after lock shows GW points
• Fixtures = live PL scores · tap for goals & assists
• Table = classic or H2H standings

————————————
NOT USING (for now)
————————————

• Extra chips for weekly wins — maybe later if the same people always lead

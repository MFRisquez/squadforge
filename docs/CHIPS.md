## Recommended chip set for 5–10 friends

Keep it familiar (like official FPL) + **one** SquadForge custom chip. More than ~5 chips and people forget what they have left.

| Chip | What it does | Uses per season (suggestion) |
|------|----------------|------------------------------|
| **Wildcard** | Unlimited free transfers **until the next deadline** (rebuild for real) | 2 — first available from the start; **second unlocks from GW20** |
| **Free Hit** | Change the whole squad for **one GW only**; after that GW your old squad returns | 1 |
| **Bench Boost** | Your bench players’ points **also count** that GW | 1 |
| **Triple Captain** | Captain scores **×3** instead of ×2 that GW | 1 |
| **Super Sub** *(custom)* | Pick **one bench player** before deadline. If they play (or auto-sub in), their points count at **×2** that GW. Does **not** stack with Triple Captain. **Chip is consumed even if they don’t play.** | 1 |

### Why not invent 8 chips?
Small leagues die from complexity. Four classic chips + one fun custom is enough drama.

## About your ideas (mapped)

- “Switch entire team one GW no cost” → **Free Hit**
- “Change entire team no cost” (permanent rebuild) → **Wildcard**
- “Bench that also plays” → **Bench Boost**
- “Sub enters with double points” → **Super Sub** (cleaner than doubling *every* auto-sub)
- Triple captain → **Triple Captain**

## Manager / “wins” related chips — opinion

| Idea | Verdict |
|------|---------|
| Extra chip when you win the weekly league | Fun, but snowballs leaders. Skip for v1 |
| “Revenge” chip after finishing last in a GW | Nice catch-up fantasy — optional later |
| Head-to-head challenge week | Needs H2H structure; add only if you want mini-cups |
| Steal 0.5m / block a transfer | Feels toxic in a friend group | Skip |
| **Once-per-season “underdog”**: if you’re bottom at GW15, gain an extra Free Hit | Soft catch-up — maybe later |

**Recommendation:** launch with the 5 chips in the table. Add a catch-up chip only if the same 1–2 people dominate by Christmas.

## Simple stacking rules (important)

1. Only **one chip** active per manager per GW (except Wildcard can overlap a period with nothing else — same as FPL: WC and Free Hit are alternative rebuild tools).
2. **Triple Captain** replaces captain ×2 that week (not ×2 then ×3).
3. **Super Sub** does not apply to the captain in the XI; only the chosen bench player.
4. **Bench Boost** + **Super Sub**: don’t allow same GW (pick one chip).
5. Auto-subs still work as normal when Bench Boost is off.
6. No official FPL “BPS bonus” in v1 (keeps scoring explainable).

Rules

Quick reference for scoring, transfers, DT, and chips.

Squad & transfers

  • Squad size15 (2·5·5·3)
  • Budget£100m
  • Max per club3
  • GW1 transfersUnlimited
  • Each later GW+1 FT (max 5)
  • Extra transfer−4 hit
  • Deadline locks transfers, XI shape, and chips for that GW.

Starting XI

  • Pick each GWXI · bench · C · V
  • Captain×2
  • If C plays 0 minsVice gets ×2
  • Injured / suspended in XIName in red
  • Still in XI at deadline−1 each
  • After the deadline, XI/bench are locked — but you may change captain/vice to a starter whose match has not started yet (e.g. Friday captain locks at kickoff; Saturday you can move the armband).
  • Autosubs can bring a bench player in if someone in your XI doesn’t play.

Technical Director (DT)

  • WhereSquad & XI pitch · bottom-right corner
  • Window3 gameweeks
  • Win / Draw / Loss+3 / +1 / −1
  • Same club twice in a rowNot allowed
  • Shown with the club badge (not a shirt). You can change the DT until the deadline of the first GW in that window (including before the season starts). After that the club is locked for the 3 GWs. Next window must be a different club than the one you just finished; later windows can reuse older clubs.
  • Scouting bonus: only you own a player and they score/assist/CS (GK/DEF) → +2 that GW.

SquadForge · v0.4

Rules

Private Fantasy Premier League scoring for the group.

Squad & transfers

  • Squad of 15: 2 GK · 5 DEF · 5 MID · 3 ATT
  • Budget £100m · max 3 players per Premier League club
  • GW1: unlimited free transfers
  • After GW1: +1 free transfer each GW (banked, max 5)
  • Extra transfers beyond FTs: −4 hit each
  • Deadline locks transfers, XI, and chips for that GW

XI

  • Each GW set Starting XI, bench, Captain (C) and Vice (V)
  • Captain scores ×2
  • If captain plays 0 minutes → vice gets the armband
  • Autosubs can bring a bench player in if someone in your XI doesn’t play

Appearance (all positions)

  • 2 pts if they play 60'+ minutes
  • 2 pts if they play any minutes AND get a goal or assist
  • 1 pt if they played but none of the above
  • 0 if they didn’t play

Goal/assist points are extra on top of appearance.

Cards / mistakes

  • Yellow card: −1
  • Red card: −3
  • Own goal: −2

By position

Goalkeeper

  • Clean sheet: +4
  • Goals conceded: −1 per 2
  • Saves: +1 per 3
  • Goal +6 · Assist +3
  • Penalty saved +5 · missed −2

Defender

  • Goal +6 · Assist +3 · CS +4
  • Goals conceded: −1 per 2
  • Defensive extras max +4
  • 5+ tackles +2 · 4+ ints +1 · 3+ blocks +1 · 10+ clearances +1

Midfielder

  • Goal +5 · Assist +3 · CS +1
  • Extras max +3
  • 4+ key passes +2 · 5+ tackles +1 · 4+ ints +1

Attacker

  • Goal +4 · Assist +3
  • Extras max +3
  • 3+ shots on target +2 · 6+ shots +1

Scouting bonus (+2)

If only you own a player in the league and they score, assist, or keep a CS as GK/DEF → +2 that GW.

Technical Director

  • Pick a PL club when you don’t have an active TD window
  • Scores for exactly 3 gameweeks: Win +3 · Draw +1 · Loss −1
  • Locked during the window · can’t repeat the previous club

Chips (1 per GW)

  • Wildcard (×2) — unlimited transfers until deadline (2nd from GW20)
  • Free Hit (×1) — temp squad for one GW, then restores
  • Bench Boost (×1) — bench points count
  • Triple Captain (×1) — captain ×3
  • Super Sub (×1) — one bench pick ×2 if they play

Official FPL bonus points (BPS) are not used. Questions → ask Manuel.
